## Interpretation and boundaries

The concept reflects the site's premium, personal, custom-gifting direction without cloning the theme. The lettering is an independent text treatment, not the official logo. Gift illustrations are hand-authored SVG; no storefront images or proprietary assets are bundled. The copy is original. Canada/CAD context is supported by the public site, but every demo price is fictional. We do not reproduce shipping rates, promised turnaround times, endorsements or claims of commercial results.

Public HTML/CSS inspection does not establish merchant approval, current Shopify API contracts or authoritative catalog eligibility. Before production, obtain approved brand assets and structured catalog data, map actual variants and bundle rules, and verify the experience with the merchant in a development store. Product availability and fulfillment must be checked at the time of purchase.
